How the model works

Each week, planned production is scheduled into the future by each supplier’s lead time and reduced by its yield. Arrivals plus on-hand inventory become available supply. The model allocates that supply against new demand and any backorders using the chosen policy. Unfilled backorder demand carries forward; unfilled lost-sale demand is recorded as lost. Fill rate = shipped ÷ new demand; captured margin = shipped × segment margin.

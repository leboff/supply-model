MODEL DOCUMENTATION

How the model works

Each week, planned production is scheduled into the future by each supplier’s lead time and reduced by its yield. Arrivals plus on-hand inventory become available supply.

The model allocates available supply against new demand and any backorders using the chosen policy. Unfilled backorder demand carries forward; unfilled lost-sale demand is recorded as lost.

Fill rate equals shipped units divided by new demand. Captured margin is shipped units multiplied by the segment margin.
